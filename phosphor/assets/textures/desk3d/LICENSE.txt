CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain) Texture & HDRI Sources
========================================================
All photographic PBR maps and studio HDRI probes in this directory are sourced
from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) under the CC0 1.0 Universal license:

- desk: oak_veneer_01 — https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01 (CC0 1.0)
- wall: plastered_wall_02 — https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_02 (CC0 1.0)
- hdri: studio_small_08 — https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_08 (CC0 1.0)

Notes:
- chassis_albedo.jpg, chassis_normal.jpg, and chassis_roughness.jpg are seamless
  matte late-80s warm-beige ABS plastic derivatives of Poly Haven CC0 plastered_wall_02.
- aluminum_albedo.jpg, aluminum_normal.jpg, and aluminum_roughness.jpg are seamless
  horizontally brushed anodized aluminum derivatives of Poly Haven CC0 plastered_wall_02.
- C1-170 faceplate silkscreen markings are composited at runtime over aluminum_albedo.jpg.
